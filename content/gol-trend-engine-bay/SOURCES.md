- Confirm the 2010 manual and coolant type, brake fluid grade and power steering fluid specification.
- Confirm whether power steering uses tank marks or a cap dipstick.
- Verify the brake reservoir's asserted MIN/MAX marks and the coolant tank's exact markings.
- Verify fuse-box location, cover and layout for this equipment.

Procedures defer fluid choice to the owner's manual; they do not establish an unconfirmed product specification.
