# Engine bay knowledge

What the instructor may say about each part of the 2010 Volkswagen Gol Trend 1.6 8V petrol engine bay, beyond the pack's summary and details.
Every claim below is either general manufacturer guidance that holds for any car of this kind, or sourced to this generation of the Gol.
Fluid grades, capacities, service intervals and the fuse map of this exact car could not be confirmed without its owner's manual, so they are left out on purpose.
When asked for one of them, the instructor says to check the owner's manual, or to ask a Volkswagen dealer, who can confirm it from the vehicle identification number (VIN).

Before any check, park on level ground, apply the parking brake, switch off, support the bonnet and let the bay cool. [VW safety]
Keep hands, clothing and tools clear of the belts and the radiator fan, which can start on its own even with the ignition off. [VW Gol manual]

## Coolant reservoir

### What it is

It is the expansion tank of the engine's cooling system. [HELLA tanks]
In this bay it is the translucent pink tank with the blue cap.

### What it does

The tank gives the coolant room to expand as the engine warms up, and takes it back as the engine cools. [HELLA tanks]
The water pump circulates coolant through the engine, and the radiator passes the heat it picked up to the air. [HELLA cooling]
The thermostat decides when coolant flows through the radiator, and the fan adds airflow when the car is slow or stopped. [HELLA cooling]
Additives in the coolant also protect the system against corrosion and freezing. [Gates coolant]

### How it is built

The tank has a filler neck, a cap and hose connections, and no moving parts. [Behr Hella]
In a pressurised system like this one, valves in the cap release excess pressure and let air back in as the coolant cools. [Behr Hella]

### How to check it

1. Wait until the engine is completely cold. [VW safety]
2. Read the level through the tank wall, without opening the cap. [VW safety]
3. The level should sit between the MIN and MAX marks. [VW safety]
4. Look at the tank, the cap and the hose joints for wet spots or dried crust, which point to a leak. [Behr Hella]
5. If the level keeps dropping, have the system inspected instead of topping up again and again. [Behr Hella]

If the tank is empty, the engine has overheated, or steam is coming out, do not drive the car; get help first. [VW safety]

### Common faults

A cracked tank or a leaking hose joint lowers the level and leaves coolant around the joint. [Behr Hella]
A faulty pressure cap can cause coolant loss, excess pressure or overheating. [Behr Hella]
A low level does not prove the tank is at fault, because a leak anywhere in the circuit looks the same. [Behr Hella]
Oil, sludge or other dirt in the coolant needs a workshop check of the whole system. [Behr Hella]

### Maintenance

Never choose coolant by its colour, and never mix products that are not confirmed to be compatible. [Gates coolant]
After a flush, refill or repair, the system has to be bled to remove trapped air. [Gates coolant]

### Specifications

The coolant type, mix ratio, capacity and change interval are in the owner's manual, or a Volkswagen dealer can confirm them from the VIN.

### Safety

Never open the cap while the engine is hot, because pressurised coolant and steam can cause severe burns. [VW safety]
Keep coolant in its labelled container, away from children and animals, and clean up spills. [VW safety]
Never pour engine oil, steering fluid or brake fluid into this tank.

## Power steering reservoir

### What it is

It is the fluid tank of the hydraulic power steering. [HELLA steering]
In this bay it is the dark square tank with the yellow-green cap, next to the coolant tank.

### What it does

A pump driven by the engine draws fluid from the tank, sends it through the steering gear and back. [HELLA steering]
When you turn the wheel, that hydraulic pressure helps move the steering, so it takes less effort. [HELLA steering]
The tank only stores the fluid; the pump makes the pressure. [HELLA steering]
Losing fluid, or air getting into the circuit, weakens the assistance and can damage the pump. [HELLA steering]

### How it is built

The reservoir has a cap, an outlet that feeds the pump and an inlet for the fluid returning from the steering. [ZF steering]
Some systems also have a filter, which is replaced when the pump is repaired. [HELLA steering]

### How to check it

1. Check it with the engine stopped.
2. Look at the tank, its hose joints and the pump for leaks. [HELLA steering]
3. Read the level against the marks on the tank or on the cap, as the owner's manual describes.
4. If the level is low, have the system checked for a leak before topping up. [HELLA steering]

### Common faults

Heavy steering, a whining pump, visible leaks and foaming fluid are signs of trouble in the system. [HELLA steering]
A worn drive belt, dirty fluid or a worn pump can also weaken the assistance, so the tank is only one possible cause. [HELLA steering]
A leak, or a pump that no longer moves fluid properly, needs a workshop. [ZF steering]

### Maintenance

Never top up with a generic steering fluid or with automatic transmission fluid unless it is the approved one. [ZF steering]
Any repair that opens the circuit needs the manufacturer's refill and bleeding procedure. [ZF steering]

### Specifications

The approved fluid, capacity and change interval are in the owner's manual, or a Volkswagen dealer can confirm them from the VIN.

### Safety

Never run the pump with an empty tank, because it is lubricated by the fluid and gets damaged without it. [HELLA steering]
Never hold the steering wheel at full lock, because that puts the system at maximum pressure. [HELLA steering]
Leave hot fluid checks, bleeding and work near a running pump to a workshop. [ZF steering]

## Brake fluid reservoir

### What it is

It is the supply tank of the brake master cylinder. [SAE brake reservoir]
In this bay it is the white reservoir with the yellow cap.

### What it does

The master cylinder turns the force on the pedal into hydraulic pressure, and the brake fluid carries that force to the brakes at each wheel. [HELLA brake fluid]
As the pads wear, the caliper pistons sit further out and hold more fluid, so the level in the tank falls slowly. [HELLA brake fluid]
Brake fluid absorbs moisture over time, which lowers its boiling point. [ATE brake fluid]
Under heavy braking that moisture can boil into vapour bubbles, which compress and can cause a serious loss of braking. [HELLA brake fluid]

### How it is built

A plastic master cylinder reservoir is made of the tank, a cap and a diaphragm, and it may carry a fluid level sensor. [SAE brake reservoir]
The fluid flows from the tank's outlets into the master cylinder below it. [SAE brake reservoir]
The tank only stores fluid; the pressure is made in the master cylinder. [HELLA brake fluid]

### How to check it

1. Check it with the engine stopped and the bay cool.
2. Read the level through the reservoir wall, keeping the cap closed. [HELLA brake fluid]
3. The level should sit between the MIN and MAX marks. [VW Gol manual]
4. Look for wet spots around the reservoir and notice any recent change in how the pedal feels.
5. If the level is below MIN, has dropped suddenly, or the pedal feels soft or sinks, do not drive until the brakes have been checked. [HELLA brake fluid]

A level check does not replace a check of the brakes themselves or a test of the fluid's condition. [ATE brake fluid]

### Common faults

A leak at the reservoir, a seal or anywhere in the brakes lowers the level and can let air into the system. [HELLA brake fluid]
A slow drop can come from pad wear, but it should still be checked, not just topped up. [HELLA brake fluid]
Old fluid that has absorbed moisture can fail under heat even when the level looks right. [ATE brake fluid]
The colour of the fluid does not tell whether it is still safe. [ATE brake fluid]

### Maintenance

The fluid is changed and the brakes are bled by a workshop, with fresh fluid of the approved grade. [HELLA brake fluid]

### Specifications

The approved brake fluid grade and change interval are in the owner's manual, or a Volkswagen dealer can confirm them from the VIN.

### Safety

Brake fluid damages paint, so clean any spill at once. [HELLA brake fluid]
Keep dirt and other fluids out of the reservoir, and keep the fluid container closed so it does not absorb moisture. [ATE brake fluid]
Never top up to hide a loss of fluid that has no explanation.

## Battery

### What it is

It is the 12 volt starter battery, which stores energy to start the engine and to power the electrics. [VARTA how]
In this bay it is the black battery below the air box.

### What it does

It supplies the large current the starter motor needs, and it powers the ignition and the electronics. [VARTA how]
The alternator recharges it while the engine runs, but short trips may not give it enough time to recharge fully. [VARTA how]
The fuses protect the circuits it feeds from too much current. [Littelfuse]
A slow start can come from the battery, its connections, the starter or the charging system, so it is not a diagnosis by itself. [HELLA starter] [HELLA alternator]

### How it is built

A lead-acid battery holds lead plates, separators between them, and sulphuric acid as the electrolyte. [VARTA how]
Its cells are joined inside an acid-resistant plastic case, with two terminals for the cables. [VARTA how]
Some batteries hold free liquid electrolyte and others soak it into glass fibre mats, so the type decides what maintenance applies. [VARTA how]
A replacement must match the size, the terminal layout, the electrical rating and the mounting. [VARTA replacement]

### How to check it

1. Switch off everything electrical, and wear eye protection near the battery. [Yuasa]
2. Read its label without removing it or loosening the terminals.
3. Check the case for cracks, swelling or leaks, and stop if it is damaged. [VARTA testing]
4. Look for corrosion on the terminals, damaged cable insulation or a loose connection, keeping metal tools away from the terminals. [VARTA care] [Yuasa]
5. If starting is weak or the battery keeps going flat, have a workshop test the battery and the charging system. [VARTA testing] [HELLA alternator]

Never open a sealed, maintenance-free battery to check or add liquid. [VARTA how]

### Common faults

Corroded terminals reduce how well the battery delivers current. [VARTA care]
Being left undercharged for long damages the plates and weakens starting. [VARTA how]
A leaking or damaged case means the battery must be replaced, not repaired. [VARTA testing]
A battery that keeps going flat needs a check of the electrical system before it is replaced. [HELLA alternator]

### Maintenance

Keep the outside and the terminals clean, and follow the battery maker's instructions when charging it. [VARTA care] [VARTA charging]

### Specifications

The required capacity, cold cranking rating and battery size are in the owner's manual, or a parts shop can match them from the car and the battery fitted.

### Safety

Battery acid burns skin and eyes, and charging releases explosive gas. [Yuasa]
Keep flames, sparks, cigarettes, rings and metal tools away from the terminals. [Yuasa]
When the battery must be disconnected, remove the negative terminal first and reconnect it last. [VARTA charging] [VARTA replacement]
Never disconnect the battery while the engine is running. [HELLA alternator]

## Fuse box

### What it is

It is the fuse holder that sits on top of the battery. [VW Gol manual]
It groups protective fuses and their connections in one place. [OptiFuse]

### What it does

A fuse cuts a circuit when too much current melts the metal link inside it. [Littelfuse]
That protects the circuit from an overload or a short circuit, so a blown fuse is also a sign to look for the fault behind it. [Littelfuse]
The battery supplies the power, and each fuse protects one of the feeds that leave it. [Littelfuse]

### How it is built

A fuse holder has an insulating body, metal contacts and cable connections around the fuses. [OptiFuse]
Fuses can plug in or bolt down, and a replacement must be the same type and rating. [Littelfuse]

### How to check it

1. Switch off everything electrical and keep the area dry. [VW Gol manual]
2. With the engine stopped and the bay cool, look at the cover for damage, a loose fit or signs of overheating.
3. If you see melted plastic, corrosion or damaged wiring, stop and have an auto electrician check it. [OptiFuse] [Littelfuse]

Volkswagen leaves the fuses on top of the battery to a workshop. [VW Gol manual]
The fuses an owner may change are in the fuse box inside the cabin, which the owner's manual describes. [VW Gol manual]

### Common faults

A blown fuse leaves the function it protects completely dead. [Littelfuse]
Corroded or loose contacts can cut the connection or make it heat up. [OptiFuse]
A new fuse that blows again means a fault that needs diagnosis. [Littelfuse]

### Specifications

The fuse map and ratings are in the owner's manual.

### Safety

Never fit a bigger fuse, a wire or foil in place of a fuse, because that removes the protection. [Littelfuse]
Keep tools away from the battery feeds, and do not assume the block is dead with the ignition off. [Yuasa]
Keep the cover on. [VW Gol manual]

## Engine

### What it is

It is a 1.6 litre petrol engine of Volkswagen's EA111 family, which this Gol Trend used from 2008 to 2011. [Marelli]
It has four cylinders, a single overhead camshaft driven by a timing belt, and two valves per cylinder. [Gates catalogue]
The valve cover and the intake manifold are parts of it.

### What it does

The engine draws in air and fuel, compresses them, ignites them with a spark and pushes out the exhaust. [DOE engine basics]
The burning gases push the pistons, which turn the crankshaft and send power through the gearbox to the wheels. [DOE engine basics]
The battery and the starter turn it over to start, and the intake supplies the air it burns. [VARTA how] [DOE engine basics]
Engine oil protects the moving parts, and the coolant carries away heat. [Mobil car care] [HELLA cooling]

### How to check it

1. Do a visual check with the engine stopped and cold.
2. Look for fresh oil or coolant on the visible parts, without removing covers. [Fel-Pro leaks] [Behr Hella]
3. Look for damaged hoses or loose wiring, without pulling on them.
4. To read the oil, with the car level and the engine off, pull the dipstick, wipe it clean, push it fully back in, then pull it again and read the level between the marks. [Mobil dipstick]
5. If oil is needed, add a little of the approved oil through the filler cap and read again, never above the top mark. [Mobil dipstick]
6. Refit the dipstick and the filler cap, and have the engine checked if it keeps losing oil. [Mobil dipstick] [Fel-Pro leaks]

Never reach into a running or just-stopped engine.

### Common faults

Low or dirty oil causes wear, overheating and can destroy the engine. [Mobil car care] [Mobil dipstick]
A cooling fault can raise the temperature even when the coolant tank looks fine. [HELLA cooling]
A rough idle, hesitation or lack of power can come from air leaks, the ignition or a mechanical problem. [Motorservice intake]
The oil pressure warning light means stop and switch off at once, even if the oil level is right. [VW safety]

### Specifications

The engine oil grade is 5W-40, as the owner's manual states. [Owner manual]
The oil capacity, oil and filter interval, spark plugs and timing belt interval are in the owner's manual and service booklet, or a Volkswagen dealer can confirm them from the VIN.

### Safety

Keep clear of the belts and the fan even with the engine stopped.
Do not touch ignition wiring or hot surfaces. [VW Gol manual]
Never run the engine in a closed space, because the exhaust can poison you. [VW safety]

## Valve cover

### What it is

It is the cover that closes the top of the cylinder head over the valve mechanism. [Fel-Pro cover]
It is made of cast aluminium, and the oil filler cap is on it. [VW Gol training]

### What it does

Its seal keeps the engine oil inside the top of the engine. [Fel-Pro cover]
On this engine it also forms the upper half of the camshaft bearings, so it is a structural part, not just a lid. [VW Gol training]
The crankcase ventilation carries away the gases that leak past the pistons, and a fault there can push oil out at the seals. [Motorservice oil]

### How it is built

The cover and the cylinder head are machined as a matched pair, and their joint is sealed with liquid sealant rather than a rubber gasket. [VW Gol training]
Too much sealant can block the camshaft's oil passages, so the joint must be sealed exactly as Volkswagen specifies. [VW Gol training]

### How to check it

1. Leave the engine off until the cover is cool.
2. Look along the seam around the cover for fresh oil or a wet trail of dirt. [Fel-Pro leaks]
3. Check around the filler cap before blaming the seam, because spilled oil from filling looks the same. [Fel-Pro leaks]
4. Note where the fresh oil starts, because oil runs away from where it leaks. [Fel-Pro leaks]
5. Check the oil level as in the engine section, and have a leak that keeps coming back checked. [Mobil dipstick] [Fel-Pro leaks]

Leave the cover and its bolts in place.

### Common faults

A leaking joint leaves oil along the edge of the cover or running down the engine. [Fel-Pro leaks]
Spilled oil and leaks from other seals can look the same until the source is found. [Fel-Pro leaks]
A joint resealed with the wrong amount or placement of sealant can leak again. [Elring sealant]
Too much pressure in the crankcase can push oil past good seals, so the ventilation may need checking too. [Motorservice oil]

### Maintenance

Resealing it needs the specified surface preparation, bead placement and amount of sealant. [Elring sealant]

### Safety

Never loosen the cover to look at the valves, because it holds the camshaft bearings. [VW Gol training]
Never smear extra sealant on the outside instead of finding and fixing the leak. [Elring sealant]
Pour only the approved engine oil through the filler, and keep dirt out. [Mobil dipstick] [Mobil car care]

## Intake manifold

### What it is

It is the part that spreads the incoming air to the engine's cylinders. [Mobil car care]
It is made of polyamide plastic reinforced with glass fibre, and it carries the throttle body and the intake pressure and temperature sensor. [VW Gol training]

### What it does

Air comes in through the air filter, passes the throttle and is shared between the cylinders. [Mobil car care]
The throttle controls how much air enters, and the pressure sensor tells the engine computer how much air the engine is taking in. [Motorservice intake]

### How to check it

1. Check it with the engine stopped and the bay cool.
2. Look at the manifold, the duct joints and the hoses for cracks, loose clamps or disconnected hoses. [Motorservice intake]
3. Check that the electrical connectors are seated, without unplugging the sensors.
4. Note any rough idle, hesitation or warning light, and have a workshop look for air leaks and read the fault codes. [Motorservice intake]

Never start the engine with intake parts removed.

### Common faults

A leaking seal or hose lets in air the engine does not measure, which causes an unstable idle, jerking when accelerating or a loss of power. [Motorservice intake]
A dirty throttle, a wrong pressure reading or another vacuum leak can cause the same symptoms. [Motorservice intake]
A pressure fault code does not prove the sensor itself has to be replaced. [Motorservice intake]

### Maintenance

Keep the air filter and the ducts after it sealed, because dirt that gets in wears the engine. [Mobil car care]

### Safety

Never spray flammable products around the engine to hunt for an air leak.
Leave running-engine tests, smoke tests and throttle work to a workshop.
Keep tools and debris out of any intake opening, and refit everything before starting. [Mobil car care]
