# Splat Field Guide

A maintenance guide for real equipment that runs on a phone with no network.
It shows a photoreal Gaussian-splat capture of the equipment, highlights any part you touch, walks you through procedures step by step with the right parts highlighted, and answers spoken questions through an on-device AI instructor.

Status: building, Apple first.
What it must do is in [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md); progress is in [TASKS.md](TASKS.md).
